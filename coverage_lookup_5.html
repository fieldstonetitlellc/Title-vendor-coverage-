Important notes & exceptions
  • Maryland: J&L copies are not included. No Judgment copies — only the judgment summary.
  • New York: Coverage is the 5 Boroughs plus IQS counties (client subscription required).
  • Arkansas: Client subscription required to access search products.
  • Texas: Tax Search only. Tarrant, Denton, and Collin are partial coverage.
  • California: Tax Search only — no other search products available.
  • North Carolina: VCAP search is not available.
  • Unofficial copies of documents are provided as part of the search package.
  • Paid county subscriptions must be provided by the client (except for Georgia).
